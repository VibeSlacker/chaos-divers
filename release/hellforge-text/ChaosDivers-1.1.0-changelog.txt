1.1.0: A harder, more chaotic fight, and you choose how hard. The intel is now picked 60 to 250 m from your squad's start, never on top of you, and a re-roll key (bindings page, MODS > CHAOS DIVERS > Re-roll Intel; unbound by default) throws away an intel you cannot reach and picks another. Two new sliders on the MODS options page: HOSTILE SEAF (PERCENT), default 50, and MAX HOSTILE SEAF AT ONCE, default 30. SEAF that drop together turn, or stay loyal, as one squad. A squad that is to turn waits until it has landed, so the loyal SEAF do not shoot it out of the sky and give the game away (drop_wait, default 7 seconds). SEAF already standing there when you take the intel turn at once.
