CHAOS DIVERS: DISSIDENT DISCOVERY
Backstory, by vibeslacker

THE STORY

It starts as a routine drop.

Then the truth comes out. The SEAF squads on the ground are enemy
sympathisers. They are the ones who have been building the broadcast
towers and sabotaging Super Earth from the inside.

Caught in the open, and too arrogant to hide it any longer, they turn
their guns on the Helldivers. They fight alongside whoever holds the
planet: the Illuminate on squid worlds, the Terminids on bug worlds,
the Automatons on bot worlds.

Command is behind, lacking the intel. It still calls in SEAF as
friendly support and still lists them as allies on the comms. They are
not. Every Pelican that lands brings more dissidents to the fight.


COMMS BLACKOUT

The broadcast towers were never just propaganda. Every one the
dissidents raised is a relay in a jamming net, and the moment the cell
is exposed they light it up.

Your uplink to the Super Destroyer drops to static. No voice, no data,
no kill reports going up.

The towers do worse than jam. They spoof the feed. Every SEAF
transponder on the planet still broadcasts a clean friendly tag, so
what the Destroyer sees from orbit is a squad of loyal troops fighting
alongside you. That is why command keeps sending Pelicans full of
"reinforcements." That is why no penalty comes down when a traitor in
SEAF armor drops to your rifle. As far as the ship can tell, nothing
happened.

Only one thing gets through: the stratagem beacon. It is a hardened
short burst, coordinates only and nothing else, and it punches through
the noise. The Destroyer will still drop what you ask for. It just has
no idea who you are aiming it at.

You are alone down here with nobody to warn and nobody to listen.

Complete the mission.


IN GAME

When rebel mode switches on, the screen shows:

    DISSIDENT CELL DISCOVERED
    SEAF FORCES HAVE TURNED HOSTILE.

Rebel SEAF fight with the enemy holding the map. Your guard dog and
sentries stay on your side and engage the rebels. Killing rebels does
not mark you as a traitor or bring the destroyer down on you.
